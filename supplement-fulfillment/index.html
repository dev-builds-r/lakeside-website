Questions

Straight Answers

How much does supplement fulfillment cost?

Most supplement orders fall in our light or medium pick-and-pack tier depending on bottle size and weight. Our published ranges are on the pricing page, and we send an exact quote for your product within 24 hours.

Do you track lots and expiration dates?

We rotate stock first-expired, first-out and track expiration dates so the oldest product ships first.

Can you ship both my Shopify and Amazon orders?

Yes. Amazon FBM orders are picked and shipped from the same inventory as your Shopify orders, at the same rate. We also prep and send inventory in to Amazon FBA, priced per unit.

How fast do orders ship?

Orders received by 2 PM CST ship the same day. From Austin, Texas most U.S. addresses receive their order in 3 to 5 days.

Can you handle subscription orders and bundles?

Yes. We assemble bundles, starter kits and recurring subscription shipments. See kitting and assembly for details.

How do we get started?

Request a quote, and we will send pricing within 24 hours. Onboarding can be done in as little as two weeks.
