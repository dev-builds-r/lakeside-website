Why Brands Move Here

Pricing That Makes Sense for Small Vials

Peptide orders are small and light, but they are rarely one item. So we price them the way they actually ship: a flat fee per order, plus a small fee for each additional vial in the box. A one-vial order and a six-vial order are not billed the same, and neither one is billed like a five-pound parcel.

Vial labeling is priced per vial. Storage is priced per pallet per month. You get an itemized invoice that ties back to the orders on your dashboard.

For brands that want it, we also take on inventory planning: tracking sell-through by SKU and telling you when to reorder before you stock out.

See the full pricing page for our published ranges, or request a quote and we will send your exact rates within 24 hours.

Questions

Straight Answers

How is peptide fulfillment priced?

A flat fee per order plus a small fee for each additional vial in that order. Vial labeling is billed per vial and storage is billed per pallet per month. We quote every brand individually and send exact rates within 24 hours of your request.

Can you label our vials?

Yes. Send us unlabeled vials and your labels and we apply them in-house, billed per vial. Many brands keep unlabeled stock on our shelves and have us label to demand.

Will our customers see the Lakeside name?

No. Packing slips carry only your brand. Nothing in the box references Lakeside Fulfillment.

How fast do orders ship?

Orders received by 2 PM CST ship the same day. From Austin, Texas most U.S. addresses receive their order in 3 to 5 days.

Which store platforms do you work with?

Orders reach us through ShipStation, which connects to Shopify, WooCommerce and most other e-commerce platforms. Tracking numbers are sent back to your store automatically.

Can we see our inventory in real time?

Yes. Every client gets a dashboard showing current stock by SKU, shipped orders and billing.

How long does onboarding take?

Onboarding can be done in as little as two weeks from your first call to your first shipped order.

Where are you located?

Our warehouse is in Austin, Texas. More on our Austin fulfillment center.
