Questions

Straight Answers

How much does a 3PL cost per order?

At Lakeside, pick and pack runs $1.50 to $2.00 per order for light items in a poly mailer, $3.00 to $3.75 for boxed items from 8 oz to 1.5 lb, and $3.95 to $5.25 for heavy or fragile items. Each additional item in an order adds $0.50 to $0.75.

How much is storage?

$45.00 per pallet per month, flat, regardless of pallet height.

Is there a monthly minimum?

Smaller accounts have a monthly minimum of $300.00 to $350.00. If your fulfillment fees for the month are above it, the minimum never comes into play.

Is packaging included?

The standard poly mailer or box is included in the pick and pack rate. Custom branded packaging you supply is packed at no extra charge.

Is postage included?

No. Pick and pack covers labor and standard packaging. Postage is billed separately.

Are there setup fees?

A one-time onboarding fee of $150.00 to $250.00 covers account setup and connecting your store.

Do you charge more for Amazon orders?

No. Amazon FBM orders are billed at the same rate as DTC orders. Amazon FBA prep is priced per unit, $1.25 to $1.50.
