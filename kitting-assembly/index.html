Services

The Work Between Receiving and Shipping

If your product needs hands on it before it goes in a box, that is kitting. Here is what we do.

🎁

Bundles & Kits

Multi-item bundles, starter kits and gift sets assembled from your individual SKUs, either built ahead as stock or picked together at the order.

🏷️

Product Labeling

Labels applied to bottles, vials, bags and boxes, billed per unit. Relabeling for rebrands and corrections too.

📦

Subscription Boxes

Recurring boxes assembled and shipped on your cadence, with flexible configurations month to month.

✉️

Inserts & Branded Packaging

Thank-you cards, instructions, samples and custom packaging added to every order so the unboxing looks like your brand.

🛒

Amazon FBA Prep

Poly bagging, FNSKU labeling and case packing, priced per unit, then shipped in to Amazon.

🏬

Retail & Wholesale Prep

Case packs, compliance labels and palletized orders built to your retailer's routing guide.

Custom Projects

Questions

Straight Answers

How is kitting priced?

Per unit or per kit, based on how many touches the work takes. We quote after seeing the product or a clear description, and the price is fixed before work starts.

Can you label our products?

Yes. We label bottles, vials, bags and boxes in-house, billed per unit. See peptide fulfillment for how vial labeling works.

Do you do Amazon FBA prep?

Yes. We poly bag, apply FNSKU labels, case pack and ship your inventory in to Amazon, priced per unit.

Do you also ship the orders after kitting?

Yes. Kitting and fulfillment happen in the same Austin warehouse, so finished kits go straight to the shelf and ship same day when orders come in.

How do we get a quote on a custom project?

Send a description or a sample through the quote form or call (737) 343-2348. We respond within 24 hours.
